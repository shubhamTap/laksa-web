Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026 · Last updated 30 August 2026

LAKSA helps a shopkeeper track what they sell and decide what to reorder. To do that it needs to know what you stock and how much of it you sell. That is essentially all it collects. It carries no advertising, no analytics or tracking software of any kind, and it never asks for payment details.

Who we are

LAKSA is operated by Laksa Technology, a company registered in Ontario, Canada. Where data-protection law requires it, Laksa Technology is the data controller (or the equivalent term under your local law) for the information described here.

Contact us about anything on this page at privacy@laksa.app.

What we collect

Information you give us to create an account

Depending on how you sign in, this is your email address, your mobile number, or the name and email address your Google or Facebook account shares with us when you choose that sign-in method. We also store whether you run a thela or a kirana, because it decides which products the app offers you.

If you sign up with an email address and password, we store the password only as a cryptographic hash. Nobody at Laksa Technology can read it.

Information about your shop's trading

  • Which products you have chosen to stock, including any product you add yourself
  • The quantity you record as sold, and the date you record it against
  • Orders you place through the app, and what you actually received
  • Stock counts you enter when the app asks you to recount something
  • Figures the app calculates from the above — estimated stock on hand, expected supplier lead time, forecasts and reorder suggestions

Technical information

  • A push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications. It identifies the device to Google's messaging service so a reminder can reach it. It is not a device fingerprint and we do not use it to identify you anywhere else.
  • Anything you type into the Report a problem box, and ordinary server logs kept for security and debugging.
What we deliberately do not collect. No prices, revenue or profit figures — the app is built to work without them. No information about your customers. No location or GPS data. No contacts, photos, camera or microphone access. No advertising identifiers. No analytics, crash reporting or tracking software is built into the app at all. And no bank, card or payment details: LAKSA never asks for them, and never will through the app.

Why we use it, and what we never do with it

Your trading data exists for one purpose: to work out what you are likely to sell and tell you what to reorder before you run out. Your account details exist so you can sign in and so we can send you a verification code or, if you allow it, a reminder to log the day's sales.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers. We do not show your shop's data to any other user. Products you create yourself and every sale you record are visible only to your own account.

We may publish or share statistics that combine many shops in a way that cannot identify any individual shop or person — for example, that a category tends to sell faster in a given season. Nothing that identifies you or your shop is included.

Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are hosted in the United States, and the providers above operate internationally. If you use LAKSA from India, Canada or anywhere else, your information is transferred to and processed in countries whose data-protection laws may differ from your own. By using LAKSA you agree to that transfer. We take reasonable steps to see that it stays protected wherever it is held.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and trading history for as long as your account is open, because the forecast is built from your own sales history — a shorter window would make the app worse at its job. Verification codes are deleted shortly after they expire.

If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal details and your shop's trading data. Backups are overwritten on their ordinary cycle, and we may keep the minimum needed to meet a legal obligation.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your account and its data
  • stop sending you notifications — you can also just turn them off on your device
  • withdraw consent you have given, where we rely on consent

Write to privacy@laksa.app from the address or number on your account and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge. If you are in India you may also raise a grievance with us at that address; if you are in Canada you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Each account's data is scoped to that account on the server, so one shopkeeper cannot read another's products, sales or orders. No system is perfectly secure, but we take these obligations seriously and fix problems promptly when we find them.

Children

LAKSA is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above. If the change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
