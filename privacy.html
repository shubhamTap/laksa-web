Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026 · Last updated 9 November 2026

LAKSA collects the account, shop and trading information needed to provide the service, limited technical and usage information, and — only when you choose those features — bill images or voice recordings. LAKSA does not use advertising or third-party analytics and never asks for bank or card details.

Who we are

LAKSA is operated by Shubham Tapadar, doing business as Laksa Technology, a business registered in Ontario, Canada. Laksa Technology is a registered business name, not a separate legal entity, so where data-protection law requires it Shubham Tapadar is the data controller (or the equivalent term under your local law) for the information described here, and is personally accountable for it.

Contact us about anything on this page at privacy@laksa.app.

What we collect

Information you give us to create an account

Depending on how you sign in, this is your email address, your mobile number, or the name and email address your Google or Facebook account shares with us when you choose that sign-in method. We also store whether you run a thela or a kirana, because it decides which products the app offers you, and the language you have chosen to read LAKSA in, so that the app, its notifications and its emails reach you in that language on any phone you sign in from.

If you sign up with an email address and password, we store the password only as a cryptographic hash. Nobody at Laksa Technology can read it.

From version 1.4.0, LAKSA may use Android and Google credential services to make signing in on a replacement device easier. LAKSA keeps only the credential information needed to provide this feature, does not use it for advertising or tracking, and disables the LAKSA restore credential when you sign out. Google Play services may also offer a phone number for you to select during sign-in; LAKSA receives it only if you choose it.

Information about your shop's trading

  • Which products you have chosen to stock, including any product you add yourself
  • The quantity you record as sold, and the date you record it against
  • Orders you place through the app, and what you actually received
  • Stock counts you enter
  • Information LAKSA derives from the above to provide its stock, ordering and recommendation features

Photographs of bills, and voice recordings (from version 1.4.0)

Instead of typing, you can photograph a paper or say what happened, and the app fills the figures in for you to check. If you choose to, we collect:

  • a photograph of a supplier's bill, invoice or order slip, taken from the Orders or Receipts screen;
  • a photograph of your own sales bill or chit, taken from the Sales screen;
  • a voice recording of up to 30 seconds, in which you say what sold, what arrived, what you ordered or what you need to order.

If you use Scan or voice entry, LAKSA sends the image or recording, together with limited shop and catalog information needed to process it, to Google's paid Gemini service. We do not send your LAKSA account identifiers or your existing sales, order or stock history with that request. Under Google's current paid-service terms, content sent through the service is not used to improve Google's products.

The original image or recording and the resulting information are stored with your account so that you can review and correct the result and continue using the feature. You check every line before anything is saved; nothing is recorded from a photograph or a recording without your confirmation. Original media is deleted after one year. Limited extracted information needed for your account may remain until the account is deleted.

We do not intentionally extract or retain customer names or phone numbers from your sales bills. An original bill image you choose to upload may contain information visible on the paper until that image is deleted. A supplier's phone number read from a supplier's bill is not stored in full. Any location information your camera embeds in a photograph is removed on the phone before the photograph is sent.

Prices. Price information visible in an optional bill or recording may be stored with the scanned record. LAKSA does not currently display or otherwise use that price information.

You can stop at any time by not using the camera or the microphone. The camera is used only when you choose Scan, and the microphone only after you tap to speak, while the listening sheet is on screen; the microphone asks for permission the first time, after the app has told you what it is for. Neither is ever used in the background.

Technical information

  • A push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications. It identifies the device to Google's messaging service so a reminder can reach it. We do not use it to identify you anywhere else.
  • Anything you type into the Report a problem box. It is stored with your account so that a person can read and answer it. Our support mailbox receives only a notice that a report exists — when it was made, whether you run a thela or a kirana, and your language — not your message, your email address or your mobile number. A problem report is deleted with your account.
  • Ordinary server logs, kept for security and debugging.
  • If the app crashes, a technical crash report — the error, where in our code it happened, and basic device and app information. It carries none of your sales, your products or your account details. Described below.
  • How the app is used (from version 1.4.0). LAKSA records limited first-party information about how features are used to improve usability and reliability. It does not include product names, text you enter, sales values or quantities. This information is kept by LAKSA, is not used for advertising, and is deleted after up to 400 days or when your account is deleted.
  • Device information (from version 1.4.0). LAKSA collects limited device and app characteristics, such as Android and app version and display settings, to improve compatibility. It does not collect your IMEI or advertising identifier.
  • Region (from version 1.4.0). LAKSA may use limited SIM/operator and language information to infer a broad region for localising content. This is not GPS or precise/current location data.

What stays on your phone (from version 1.4.0)

To support weak or interrupted connections, LAKSA may temporarily keep account data and unsent entries in app-private storage on your device. This is cleared when you sign out, deactivate or delete your account. After an interrupted session, unsent entries or media may remain in app-private storage on that device for up to 30 days before being deleted.

What we deliberately do not collect. No revenue or profit figures, and no prices in anything you type; price information visible in a bill or recording you choose to upload is described above. We do not intentionally extract or retain customer names or phone numbers from your sales bills. LAKSA does not collect GPS or precise/current location; it may use limited information to infer a broad region for localisation, as described above. No contacts, and no access to your photos beyond a picture you yourself choose to import. The camera and the microphone are used only when you tap to scan or to speak, and never in the background. No advertising identifiers. No advertising or attribution software, and no third-party analytics: no Firebase Analytics, no Meta pixel. What LAKSA records about how it is used (above) goes to our own server and to nobody else, and is not used for advertising. Meta's app-event logging and advertising-ID collection — which its sign-in library switches on by default — are turned off. And no bank, card or payment details: LAKSA never asks for them, and never will through the app.

The one thing here that goes to a third party is crash reports, added on 9 September 2026 and described below. We would rather name it here than let this list imply otherwise.

Two things are unavoidable and we would rather name them than let the list above imply otherwise. Signing in and receiving notifications require the app to hold identifiers issued by Google — a per-installation ID and a notification token — and Google receives them. If you sign in by SMS, Google also runs an automated check on the device to confirm the request is genuine and not a bot. Neither is used to profile you, and neither is an advertising identifier, but both are real and they leave your device. From version 1.4.0, Android and Google credential services may hold the restore credential described above, and Google Play services may offer a phone number on the sign-in screen; both run on your phone under Google's terms.

When the app crashes, it sends a report. That report says what went wrong — the error and the point in our code it came from — along with basic device and app information. It does not contain your sales, your products, your email address or your phone number. Technical crash reports are handled separately by Firebase Crashlytics and are not linked by LAKSA to your account. They are retained according to Firebase's applicable retention schedule. We use them only to find and fix the fault.

Why we use it, and what we never do with it

We use your trading data to provide LAKSA's inventory, ordering and recommendation features. We use account information to sign you in and communicate with you, and limited usage and device information to improve reliability and compatibility. We may also use aggregated or de-identified information to improve LAKSA.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not show your shop's data to any other user. Products you create yourself and every sale you record are visible only to your own account.

We may publish or share statistics that combine many shops in a way that cannot identify any individual shop or person — for example, that a category tends to sell faster in a given season. Nothing that identifies you or your shop is included.

Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are hosted in the United States, and the providers above operate internationally. If you use LAKSA from India, Canada or anywhere else, your information is transferred to and processed in countries whose data-protection laws may differ from your own. Creating an account means agreeing to this policy, including that transfer. If you would rather your information were not processed outside your own country, do not create an account; if you change your mind later, you can delete your account in the app, or ask us to delete it. We take reasonable steps to see that it stays protected wherever it is held.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and trading history for as long as your account is open, because LAKSA's features are built from your own history — a shorter window would make the app worse at its job. Verification codes, and the email address or number each was sent to, are deleted within a day of expiring. From version 1.4.0: an original bill image or recording is deleted one year after it was made, and limited extracted information needed for your account may remain until the account is deleted; usage information is deleted after up to 400 days; device and region information is replaced whenever it changes; the restore credential is disabled when you sign out.

You can close your account two different ways, and they are not the same.

  • Deactivate (Settings → Deactivate account) simply switches the account off. Your sales, orders, inventory and history stay exactly as they are, and signing in again reactivates it. Nothing is deleted.
  • Delete (Settings → Delete account) erases it. Everything associated with your LAKSA account — your account and sign-in information; shop, product, sales, order and stock data; account-linked information created by LAKSA; optional bill images and voice recordings and information extracted from them; account-linked usage, device and regional information; any problem reports you sent; and notification or sign-in credentials — is deleted from LAKSA's active systems, along with the sign-in record Google's Firebase keeps for us if you signed in with Google, Facebook or your phone. This cannot be undone, and we ask you to type DELETE to confirm because we cannot get it back for you afterwards. The email address or phone number becomes free to use for a new account again.

Crash reports are separate, and deleting your account does not remove them. Technical crash reports are handled separately by Firebase Crashlytics and are not linked by LAKSA to your account. They are retained according to Firebase's applicable retention schedule. We would rather say this plainly than let “delete erases everything” stand when it does not.

Backups are overwritten on their ordinary cycle, and we may keep the minimum needed to meet a legal obligation. If you would rather ask us to erase your data than do it yourself — or no longer have the app installed — write to privacy@laksa.app and we will. Delete your account has the full instructions.

Where we rely on your consent

Being specific about this matters, because you cannot meaningfully withdraw consent you were never told you had given. We rely on your consent for exactly three things — and there are things we do that consent is not the honest word for. All of them are below:

  • Running the app for you. Creating an account and recording what you stock and sell, so the app can forecast and suggest reorders. This is the core of the service; without it there is nothing for LAKSA to do.
  • Push notifications. Separate, optional, and asked for by your device the first time. Declining costs you nothing else.
  • The camera and the microphone (from version 1.4.0). Separate and optional: nothing is photographed or recorded until you tap to scan or to speak, and your device asks for the microphone the first time, after the app has told you what it is for. Declining costs you only that way of entering figures; typing always works.

Crash reports are the first thing that is not separately switchable. If the app breaks on your phone it tells us, and there is no setting to turn that off today. We think that is the right trade for an app this young — a crash nobody reports is a crash nobody fixes — but you should be told it rather than discover it. It carries none of your shop's data, and it stops the moment you stop using the app.

Usage, device and region information (from version 1.4.0) are the same kind of thing, and they are not separately switchable either: there is no setting for them today. They go only to our own server, contain none of your sales or your products, and stop the moment you stop using the app.

We do not rely on consent for advertising, marketing or profiling, because we do none of those things.

Withdrawing it. Turn notifications off on your device, or in your device settings, at any time — everything else keeps working. Stop using the camera or the microphone, or take the microphone permission back in your device settings, and the app goes on working with typed figures. To withdraw consent for the rest, delete your account under Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account also deletes the personal and trading data held against it — there is no second step, and nothing to ask us for. You can instead ask us to do it, by writing to privacy@laksa.app. Withdrawing consent for the core service means you can no longer use LAKSA, because there is no version of it that works without knowing what you sell. Withdrawal is not retrospective: it does not undo processing that already happened lawfully.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • deactivate your account, keeping everything — in the app, under Settings → Deactivate account
  • erase your account and its data — in the app, under Settings → Delete account, or by writing to us. See How long we keep it above for what each one does
  • stop sending you notifications — you can also just turn them off on your device
  • withdraw consent — see Where we rely on your consent above
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to act for yourself. Write to us with their name and how to reach them.

Write to privacy@laksa.app from the address or number on your account and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can escalate. In India, you may raise privacy concerns with our Grievance Officer named below. You may also exercise any complaint or escalation rights available to you under applicable Indian data-protection law. In Canada, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored as hashes. We use account-level access controls, restricted cloud storage and app-private device storage to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take these obligations seriously and fix problems promptly when we find them.

Children

LAKSA is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
